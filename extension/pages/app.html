Keep the whole story.
Translate PDFs, ebooks, documents and text files. Files are parsed locally; extracted text is sent to your chosen translation engine.
Choose a documentPDF · EPUB · DOCX · HTML · TXT · MarkdownUp to 30 MiB. Scanned PDF pages use local OCR.
PDF export preserves the page background and adds translated text over its text regions. Bilingual output alternates original and translated pages. The exported PDF is rasterized; complex layouts and longer translations may need review.


Watch both languages.
Translate subtitles with their timing intact, then preview them alongside a local video. For websites, use Video captions from the extension popup.


Read beyond text.
Recognize text locally in an image, screenshot or comic panel, then translate it with your own agent.
English, Chinese and Japanese models are bundled. Additional packs can be added when building.
Drag on the image to select a region, or translate the whole image.
OCR runs on your device. Translated image regions use a clean background and fitted text; original lettering and artistic inpainting are not reproduced.


Follow the conversation.
Share a tab’s audio or your microphone. Your configured speech endpoint transcribes short audio segments; your translation agent translates the transcript.
Recording starts only after you click Start and grant the browser’s permission. Audio is sent to your chosen speech endpoint. Click Stop to end capture.
